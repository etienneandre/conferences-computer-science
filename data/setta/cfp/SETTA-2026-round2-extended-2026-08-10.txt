12th International Symposium on Dependable Software Engineering: Theories, Tools and Applications (SETTA 2026), to be held in Singapore, December 2–4, 2026.
**************************************************************************************************************************************

SETTA aims to bring together researchers and practitioners to advance the state of the art in formal methods and dependable software engineering, with a particular focus on bridging the gap between theory and real-world software systems.

Topics of interest include (but are not limited to):

    Requirements specification and analysis

    Formalisms for modeling, design and implementation

    Model checking, theorem proving, and decision procedures

    Scalable approaches to formal system analysis

    Formal approaches to simulation, run-time verification, and testing

    Integration of formal methods into software engineering practice

    Contract-based engineering of components, systems, and systems of systems

    Formal and engineering aspects of software evolution and maintenance

    Parallel and multicore programming

    Embedded, real-time, hybrid, probabilistic, and cyber-physical systems

    Mixed-critical applications and systems

    Formal aspects of service-oriented and cloud computing

    Safety, reliability, robustness, and fault-tolerance

    Dependability of smart software and systems

    Empirical analysis techniques and integration with formal methods

    Applications and industrial experience reports

    Software tools to assist the construction or analysis of software systems

    Trustworthy and explainable AI


We welcome:

    Original research papers

    Industrial application papers

    Position papers on emerging challenges


Submission guidelines:

    Peer-reviewed research papers will be published in the SETTA 2026 proceedings (Springer LNCS)

    Submissions must not exceed 16 pages (excluding references) and must follow LNCS formatting guidelines

    Papers must be written in English and submitted in PDF format via EasyChair

    Submissions must be original and not under review elsewhere

    SETTA 2026 employs double-blind reviewing; submissions must not contain author names or identifying information

    Each paper will be reviewed by at least two program committee members

    Accepted papers must be presented at the conference

Selected papers will be invited for extended versions in a reputable journal special issue.
