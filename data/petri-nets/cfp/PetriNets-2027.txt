                   48th International Conference on
         Application and Theory of Petri Nets and Concurrency
                  Rennes, France. June 21 – 25, 2027


The Petri Nets Conference 2027 will take place in Rennes, at the
Faculty of Economical Science.

We seek papers presenting original research on the application or
theory of Petri nets, as well as contributions addressing concurrent
systems more broadly, or applications of concurrency to system design.

Topics specific to Petri Nets:
- Analysis and synthesis, structure and behaviour of nets
- System design and model-driven development using nets
- Relationships between Petri nets and other approaches
- Net-based semantical, logical and algebraic calculi
- Higher-level net models (coloured nets, timed nets, etc.)
- Stochastic net models
- Verification and model checking using nets
- Process discovery and conformance checking
- Computer tools for nets
- Standardisation of nets
- Experience reports describing applications of nets to various domains

General Topics related to Concurrency:
- Application of concurrency to system design
- Model checking and verification of distributed systems
- Verification of infinite-state or parametric systems
- Causality/partial order theory of concurrency
- Educational issues related to concurrency
- New developments in the theory of concurrency
- Modelling of hardware and biological systems

Important Dates:
- Abstract submission: January 15, 2027
- Paper submission: January 22, 2027
- Notification: March 8, 2027
- Final version due: March 22, 2027
- Workshops and Tutorials: June 21–22, 2027
- Main Conference: June 23–25, 2027
Deadlines are AoE (Anywhere on Earth)

Two kinds of papers can be submitted:

- Regular papers (max. 20 pages excluding references) describing
original results pertaining to the development of the theory of Petri
nets and distributed and concurrent systems in general, new results
extending the applicability of Petri nets, or case studies,
application and experience reports pertinent to the practical use of
Petri nets and concurrency.

- Tool papers (max. 10 pages excluding references) describing a
computer tool based on Petri nets (not an application of the tool or
the theory behind the tool). The tool should be available for use by
other groups (but not necessarily for free). The submission should
indicate how the reviewers can get access to the tool (this must be
free). The tool will be demonstrated at the Tool Exhibition.

Submissions must use the Springer LNCS format and include line numbers
(use the lineno LATEX package).  Submit via EasyChair:
https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=petrinets2027

Workshops, Courses, Tutorials and Tools

The main conference will take place from Wednesday, June 23 to Friday,
June 25. The two days before the main conference also offer a wide
range of activities including workshops and tutorials. The Petri Net
Courses take place from Monday June 21 to Tuesday June 22. They offer
a thorough introduction to Petri nets in half-day and full-day
modules. Detailed descriptions of workshops and tutorials will be made
available via the conference website. An exhibition of Petri net tools
will take place on Thursday, June 24. It consists of informal
demonstrations for small groups/individuals, and there are no
scheduled talks.
