Submission Guidelines



All paper submissions must be in PDF and be strictly formatted according to the Submission Guidelines.

ACCESS2013 Paper Template could be downloaded here. Download and “save as” a template.

Accepted papers would be included in the conference proceedings.

The conference proceedings will be indexed in ISI proceedings which is the Conference Proceedings Citation Index, an integrated index within the web of sciences.

Submissions are not anonymous for the conference.

Authors need to submit their full papers by the 15 October 2012.

Papers are limited to a maximum of six (6) pages.

Inquiries can be sent to contact-AT-accesscongress.org

Submission



Submission must be made via the online submission system.
