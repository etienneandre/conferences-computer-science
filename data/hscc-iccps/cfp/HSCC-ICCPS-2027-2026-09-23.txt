CALL FOR PAPERS

ACM/IEEE International Conference on Cyber-Physical and Autonomous Systems (HSCC+ICCPS) 2027
Boulder, Colorado, USA | 17-20 May 2027
Part of Cyber-Physical Systems and Internet-of-Things Week (CPS-IoT Week 2027)

Conference website: https://hscc.acm.org/2027

Full call for papers: https://hscc.acm.org/2027/call-for-papers/

HSCC+ICCPS brings together researchers advancing the foundations, design,
implementation, evaluation, and deployment of cyber-physical systems,
hybrid systems, and trustworthy AI-enabled autonomous systems. The 2027
conference continues two established conference lineages as one unified event:

    30th ACM International Conference on Hybrid Systems: Computation and Control (HSCC).
    18th ACM/IEEE International Conference on Cyber-Physical Systems (ICCPS).

These iterations refer to the predecessor conference lineages.

The conference has three coordinated tracks:

    Foundations
    Systems and Applications
    AI-enabled Autonomy

Regular research papers may use up to 10 pages, excluding references, for contributions featuring mature original research and a comprehensive evaluation.

Short research papers may use up to 6 pages, including references, and welcome position papers, tool papers, and focused contributions.

Both categories use the ACM two-column conference format.

Accepted papers in both categories will appear in the conference's ACM proceedings. Reviews will be light double-anonymous.

REPEATABILITY EVALUATION

Authors of accepted Regular and Short papers are encouraged to submit supporting artifacts for voluntary, separate repeatability evaluation after paper notifications. Tool-oriented Short papers are especially encouraged to provide artifacts. Package requirements are available on the website; the submission portal and badge terminology will be announced separately.

https://hscc.acm.org/2027/authors/repeatability-evaluation/
