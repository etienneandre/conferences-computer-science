Submission  : 30th June 2025

We are pleased to invite informal presentations for the 10th edition of SynCoP, a satellite workshop of CONFEST 2025 dedicated to the synthesis of complex/continuous parameters in formal models.

SynCoP provides a platform for researchers interested in the decidability and algorithmic synthesis of parameters in systems such as automata, games, timed systems, probabilistic models, and hybrid systems. The workshop fosters the exchange of ideas and ongoing research across formal methods, verification, and synthesis communities.

# Scope (non-exhaustive)

* Synthesis in models with quantitative parameters (e.g., time, probabilities, energy)
* Parametrised verification and model checking
* Tools and applications for parameter synthesis
* Theoretical advances in parametric models
* Combinations of synthesis and learning

# Informal Presentation Submissions:

We invite short abstracts (3 pages) describing recently published works, ongoing work, preliminary results, or promising ideas. These presentations are not intended for proceedings publication and are ideal for discussing work in progress or receiving feedback from the community.
