CALL FOR PAPERS

 33rd International Conference on Real-Time Networks and Systems

5-7 November 2025, PISA, Italy

https://rtns2025.retis.santannapisa.it/

Highlights:

    Change of publisher: proceedings published through Springer LNCS

    Submissions: Switch to double blind policy

    Submissions: 3-round model with major revisions (see below)

    Conference venue:  Campus of Scuola Superiore Sant’Anna, Pisa, Italy


The 33rd International Conference on Real-Time Networks and Systems (RTNS) will be hosted in the vibrant city of Pisa, Italy, 5-7 November 2025.

RTNS is a friendly and inclusive conference that encourages submissions of both theoretical and experimental work, covering a wide range of topics in real-time and embedded systems, including, but not limited to:

    Formal specification and verification

    Hardware support for real-time systems

    Real-time applications design and evaluation

    Real-time aspects of emerging smart systems

    Real-time distributed systems

    Real-time networks

    Real-time system design and analysis

    Software technologies for real-time systems


Guidelines:

    Papers should be submitted as a PDF, following double blind review guidelines, using the LNCS paper style, respecting a page limit of 20 pages of technical content (excluding the title page and references).

    Three rounds of submissions to accommodate more high-quality research in January (closed), May, and August.


RTNS 2nd Round

    Submission Deadline: May 29, 2025

    Notification: July 24, 2025

    Camera ready: September 4, 2025


RTNS 3rd Round

    Submission Deadline: August 14, 2025

    Notification: October 9, 2025

    Camera ready: October 30, 2025


    Authors of outstanding papers will be invited to submit an extended version of their work for a special issue of Springer Real-Time Systems journal.

Submit your best work and join us in Pisa for an engaging and enlightening experience at RTNS 2025!
