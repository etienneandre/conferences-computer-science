-- ABOUT VECoS --

The VECoS conference series focuses on the analysis of computer and
communication systems, where functional and extra-functional properties
are inter-related. VECoS encourages the cross-fertilization between the
various formal verification and evaluation approaches, methods and
techniques, especially those developed for concurrent and distributed
hardware/software systems. VECoS is referenced by
https://conferences-computer.science/ and ranked as Category C by CORE
Topics and more details can be found at http://vecos-world.org/2026/
An overview of previous VECoS conferences can be found at :
http://vecos-world.org/

-- GENERAL INFORMATION ON SUBMISSIONS --

Submitted papers must be no longer than 15 pages (including
bibliography), written in English, presenting original work and not be
submitted for publication elsewhere.  Papers must be submitted
electronically in PDF format through EasyChair
(https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=vecos2026), using the LNCS
format (Springer, version 2.20 or later). Accepted papers will appear
in the conference proceedings.  At least one author of each accepted
paper must attend the conference and present the paper.

The conference proceedings will be published in the Springer-Verlag
Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series.

Authors of selected papers will be invited to submit extended and
revised versions of their work for consideration in a special issue of
an indexed international journal (ISSE: Innovations in Systems and
Software Engineering, IJCCBS: International Journal of Critical
Computer-Based Systems.
