Please consider to contribute to Valuetools 2025, which will be held 11-12 December 2025 in Glasgow.

 

More information and submission via the following website:

https://valuetools.eai-conferences.org/2025/

 

Extended deadlines:

* Abstract Submission deadline: September 12, 2025

* Full Paper Submission deadline: September 17, 2025

* Author Notification: October 27, 2025

* Camera-ready paper November 10, 2025

 

Valuetools 2025 solicits original papers that propose new methodologies
and tools for the performance and safety evaluation, and optimization
of complex systems. It brings together researchers from different
communities of Computer Science including but not limited to
Communication Networks, IoT, CPS, (Stochastic) Operations Research,
Optimization, Machine Learning, Control Theory and Manufacturing.

ValueTools 2025 welcomes regular submission papers describing novel
results, as well as short papers on vision work, work-in-progress, and
tools.

Advances in models and methods:

Stochastic processes and models
Queueing models and networks
Statistical simulation
Game theory
Machine learning and data analytics
Federated processing
Optimization and control theory
Process algebras
Markov decision processes
Model checking
Stochastic geometry

Application areas:

Data centers, distributed and cloud computing systems
Edge and fog computing systems
Critical infrastructures
Wireless networks
Smart grids
Social networks
Manufacturing systems and supply chains
Biological, economic, transportation, and healthcare systems
Machine learning and artificial intelligence systems
