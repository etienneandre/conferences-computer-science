---------------------
Call for Papers
---------------------
You are cordially invited to submit to VALUETOOLS 2019 (http://valuetools.org/), to be held in Palma de Mallorca, Spain from 12 to 15 March 2019 with Workshops on 12 March.

----------
Topics
----------
ValueTools 2019 focuses on methodologies and practices in modelling, performance evaluation and optimization of complex systems. It brings tog ether researchers from different communities including Computer Science, Networks and Telecommunications, Operations Research, Optimization, Control Theory and Manufacturing. ValueTools2019 solicits previously unpublished contributions that propose new performance evaluation methodologies and new tools.

Valuetools 2019 solicits papers describing original and novel research on, but not limited to, the following topics:

Methodologies
* Stochastic processes & models
* Queues and queueing networks
* Asymptotic analysis (mean field, fluid approximations, large deviation s)
* Discrete event simulation
* Software performance engineering
* Model-checking
* Process algebras, Petri Nets
* Optimization and Control Theory
* Game theory & algorithmic game theory
* Machine Learning and data analytics

Application Areas
* Distributed systems
* Large-scale systems
* Communication and computer systems
* Wireless networks (4G/5G, sensor, ad-hoc networks)
* Cloud and Edge/Fog computing systems
* Internet of Things
* Energy networks and Smart grids
* Social networks
* Networks security
* Biological systems
* Manufacturing systems & supply chains
* Transportation systems
---------------------------------------------------
Important dates and submission types
---------------------------------------------------

Regular papers (8 pages)  and Tool papers (6 pages) submission:

* Abstract submission: The deadline for abstracts (September 28, 2018) has been removed. Authors _do not_ have to submit an abstract before their full-papers. However, authors are strongly encouraged to submit a preliminary abstract in order to facilitate the review process. 
* Paper submission: October 19, 2018 (extended deadline)

Posters and Fast Abstracts submission (2 pages):   November 5th, 2018

Notification of acceptance:  December 10th, 2018
Camera ready: January 20th, 2019

Workshops (full or half day) and Tutorials (1-3 hours) proposal submission (2 pages):   Septem ber 15th, 2018
Notification of acceptance for Workshops and Tutorials:   September 30th, 2018

Submission guidelines are available on the conference web page: http:// valuetools.org/initial-submission/. 

Authors of a selection of best papers will be given the opportunity to submit an extended version of their work for publication in a special issue of the journal Performance Evaluation (https://www.journals.elsevier.com/performance-evaluation).
