CALL FOR PRESENTATIONS

======================
HYPER 2025
4th Workshop on Hyperproperties: Advances in Theory and Applications
https://hyperworkshop25.cispa.io/
co-located with CAV 2025
======================

The goal of HYPER 2025 is to foster the exchange of ideas on topics related to hyperproperties between researchers from the formal methods, security, machine learning, and cyber-physical systems communities and to present and discuss recent advances in theory and applications for specifying and analyzing hyperproperties. The workshop will comprise invited talks and selected presentations. For more details and a list of invited speakers, see https://hyperworkshop25.cispa.io/.

HYPER 2025 is co-located with CAV 2025, and will take place in Zagreb, Croatia, on July 21, 2025.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES


Submission link: https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=hyper2025

The HYPER workshop aims to bring together researchers interested in the broad area of hyperproperties and working in the areas of formal methods and control, cybersecurity, and machine learning. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:
Specification formalisms for hyperproperties
Algorithms for verification, synthesis, and runtime verification for hyperproperties
Information-flow control
Privacy
Fairness
Causality
Robustness
Explainability


Presentation proposals shall be submitted in the form of an *extended abstract of up to three pages* in LNCS format (not including references) via easychair. Submissions can overlap with previously published work and will be judged based on their relevance to the topic of the workshop. The workshop will have no formal proceedings.
