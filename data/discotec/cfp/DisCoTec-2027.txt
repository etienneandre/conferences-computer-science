DisCoTec 2027 is one of the major events sponsored by the International Federation for Information Processing (IFIP) and the European Association for Programming Languages and Systems (EAPLS). It gathers conferences and satellite events that cover a broad spectrum of distributed computing subjects — from theoretical foundations and formal description techniques, testing and verification methods, to language design and system implementation approaches. The three main conferences are:


* COORDINATION 2027 - 29th International Conference on Coordination Models and Languages
* DAIS 2027 - 27th International Conference on Distributed Applications and Interoperable Systems
* FORTE 2027 - 47th International Conference on Formal Techniques for Distributed Objects, Components, and Systems

DisCoTec 2027 is organised by the University of Augsburg.

Call for Satellite Events (Workshops and Tutorials)

Researchers and practitioners are invited to submit proposals for satellite events (workshops and tutorials) to be affiliated to DisCoTec 2027. We welcome proposals on topics related to distributed systems: from theoretical foundations and formal description techniques, testing and verification methods, to language design and system implementation approaches.

The satellite events (workshops and tutorials) will be held on Monday, June 14, 2027 and on Tuesday, June 15, 2027.

Please check https://www.discotec.org/2027/satellite_events for applications of satellite events.
