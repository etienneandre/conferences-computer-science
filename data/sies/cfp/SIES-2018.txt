 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 

 The IEEE symposium brings together researchers and practitioners from industry 
 and academia and provides them with a platform to report on recent developments, 
 deployments, technology trends and research results, as well as initiatives 
 related to embedded systems and their applications in a variety of industrial 
 environments.
