**** CALL FOR CONTRIBUTIONS ****

=======================================

29th ACM International Systems and Software Product Line Conference (SPLC)

https://2025.splc.net/

September 1st to 5th, 2025

Venue: Facultade de Informática, Universidade da Coruña (A Coruña, Spain)

=======================================

The ACM International Systems and Software Product Line Conference (SPLC)
is a premier forum where researchers, practitioners, and educators can
present and discuss the most recent ideas, trends, experiences, and
challenges in the area of software and systems product line engineering.
Conference events include opportunities to hear industry leaders'
real-world experiences and researchers' latest ideas and to learn from both.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Research Papers
(https://2025.splc.net/call-for-papers/call-for-research-papers/)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We invite original, theoretically or empirically validated submissions
addressing all areas of software product lines research (see the call for a
list of topics). This year particularly emphasizes ‘Data-intensive Software
Product Lines’, acknowledging the fact that modern software systems need to
manage large data sets along with highly configurable software. With this
theme, we encourage contributions that tackle data management problems
along the software product line. For instance, DevOps and orchestrating
distributed systems are crucial activities to support data-intensive
product lines.

Chairs: Sandra Greiner, José Galindo (sandra.greiner@unibe.ch;
jagalindo@us.es)

Artifact Chair: Kevin Feichtinger (kevin.feichtinger@kit.edu)

Challenges and Solutions Papers
(https://2025.splc.net/call-for-papers/call-for-challenges-and-solutions/)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We highly encourage community members to provide concrete sets of case
studies and problems that showcase SPL-related challenges. First, there is
a “call for cases” where researchers or practitioners are encouraged to
contribute a concrete case study. Second, after the accepted cases are
published, there is a “call for solutions”. Both, accepted case
descriptions and solutions, will be included in the conference proceedings.

This year, we are placing special emphasis in our call for cases on the
need for actionable benchmarks and competition, akin to those prevalent in
the machine learning and SAT communities, as developed on platforms like
Kaggle.

Chairs: Maider Azanza and Jacob Krüger (maider.azanza@ehu.eus;
j.kruger@tue.nl)

Deadlines (all AoE):
Challenge case submission: March 13, 2025
Challenge case notification: April 3, 2025
Camera-ready of challenge case: April 10, 2025
New challenge cases released: April 10, 2025
Challenge solution submission: June 12, 2025
