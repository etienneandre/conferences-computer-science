Challenges and Solutions Papers (https://variability-challenges.github.io/)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We highly encourage community members to provide concrete sets of case studies and problems that showcase SPL-related challenges.
First, there is a “call for cases” where researchers or practitioners are encouraged to contribute a concrete case study.
Second, after the accepted cases are published, there is a “call for solutions”. Both, accepted case descriptions and solutions, will be included in the conference proceedings.
This year, we are placing a special emphasis in our call for cases on the need for actionable benchmarks and competitio n, akin to those prevalent in the machine learning and SAT communities, and as developed on platforms like Kaggle.
Chairs: Mathieu Acher and Thomas Thüm <splc2024challengetrack@easychair.org>
Deadlines (all AoE):
     Challenge case submission: March 13, 2024
     Challenge case notification: April 4, 2024
     Camera-ready of challenge case: April 11, 2024
     Call for challenge solutions released: April 12, 2024
     Challenge solution submission: June 10, 2024
     Challenge solution notification: July 1, 2024
     Camera-ready of challenge solution: July 10, 2024
