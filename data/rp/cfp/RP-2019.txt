CALL FOR PAPERS

The Computer Science Department @ULB and the ICTEAM Institute @UCLouvain are excited to announce the

13th International Conference on Reachability Problems 2019.

Brussels, Belgium, September 11-13, 2019.

https://sites.uclouvain.be/rp2019/

* Important Dates

Submission deadline for regular papers: June 7th (abstracts required on may 31st). 
Notifications: July 12.

* Submission Guidelines *

There are two categories of submissions:

Regular Papers: Authors are invited to submit a draft of a full paper with at most 12 pages (excluding the bibliography) formatted according to LNCS guidelines. Authors are asked to submit their paper through EasyChair. Submission deadline: June 7.
 
Presentations: In addition to the formal presentations based on our LNCS proceedings volume, we invite researchers to submit a paper which recently appeared (or which is going to appear) in proceedings of another conference, or which has not yet been submitted. Neither the paper nor the abstract will be published in the proceedings.
