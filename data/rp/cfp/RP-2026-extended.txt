The 20th International Conference on Reachability Problems (RP'26)
will be organized by the Department of Mathematics and Statistics,
University of Turku, Finland.

The conference will take place on 21-23 October 2026.

https://math.utu.fi/rp26/

Submissions should be made through EasyChair:
