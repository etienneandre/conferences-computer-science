SAFECOMP 2021 will be hosted by the Department of Computer Science,
University of York.

https://safecomp2021.hosted.york.ac.uk/
It takes place in York, UK, from 7th to 10th September 2021 (online
options will be available).

SAFECOMP brings together experienced and diverse delegates from
industry and academia to discover and discuss the state-of-the-art in
the safety, security and reliability of critical computer systems and
applications.

The theme for 2021 is safe human-robotic and autonomous system (RAS)
interaction.

Call for Papers: 
https://safecomp2021.hosted.york.ac.uk/index.php/call-for-papers/  
We solicit two types of papers:
Research papers (up to 14 pages - should describe the research gap
that is addressed and explain how the proposed contribution advances
current knowledge)
Practical experience reports/tool descriptions (up to 8 pages - should
convey a clear message and lessons for practitioners)

Deadline
Abstract submission: 1 February 2021
Full paper submission: 15 February 2021

Workshops and tutorials
Tuesday 7 September will be a day of tutorials and workshops.
Proposals are welcomed.
Workshop and tutorial proposal submission deadline: 25 January 2021
